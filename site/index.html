Three ways to draw a floor.

Every screen in the MVP, designed three times. Not three colour schemes — three different answers to the one question the product turns on: what is the number on the home screen, and what does it mean when she goes under it?

9 screens · 3 directions Sample user: Sarah, 11 weeks, combo feeding Grounded in the design research, §-cited throughout
Start here

The decision the rest depends on

MyFitnessPal taught roughly 200 million people that the home number is remaining, that it should approach zero, and that going past it is failure. This product needs the opposite instinct: a number she must not go under. You cannot borrow the visual grammar of depletion without importing the wrong reflex along with it — so each direction takes a different position, and everything else follows from which one you pick.

A · Ledger
Subtract the floor out of the headline.
“Still to eat today: 1,260.” It counts down to zero and stops. Zero is the win state. There is no negative, no exceed, no red — you cannot feel bad about overspending a number that only measures what you still owe yourself. Borrowed from the one product that ever solved this inversion commercially.
B · The Band
Keep the brief’s ring. Fix its geometry.
The floor stops being a hairline tick and becomes a region the fill crosses out of; the safe state becomes a band, 1,800–2,300, so there is no knife edge to sit on. Least disruptive to what is already decided, and the fastest to build. Its cost: it keeps “1,460 left,” which is still budget grammar.
C · ProtectedStop making the daily number the hero.
The hero becomes “26 of the last 30 days protected” — a win that does not require the scale to move. Numbers are off by default, input is coarse by default, and the app asks for less over time. The boldest swing, and the only one that answers what actually kills retention.
The systems

Three design languages

Each direction has its own palette, type pairing and shape language, so the comparison is real rather than cosmetic. All three obey the same tone rules and the same clinical constraints.

Direction A

Ledger

Editorial and exact. A horizontal bar rather than a ring, because a labelled tick on a bar is unambiguous where a radial tick reads as a needle. Below the floor, the screen inverts in value rather than turning red.

Direction B

The Band

The brief rendered faithfully, with only the geometry corrected. Ring stays; the floor becomes a sector, the safe state becomes a band, and every state is encoded three times over — hue, geometry and icon.

Ground
Pale clay — a small, deliberate step off cream
Action

Type

Risk
Closest to the category’s house style
Direction C

Protected

The anti-tracker. Days protected instead of calories remaining, coarse input instead of grams, numbers hidden unless asked for. The palette runs on a clock — day, dusk, and a 2–5am mode built for the hour this product is actually opened.

Deviations

Where these designs argue with the brief

The brief is the source of truth on product decisions and none of these relitigate scope. These are places where the design research produced evidence that points a different way. Each is yours to accept or reject — flagged here rather than buried in a mock.

ChangeWhyAdopted by
Invert the headline numberThe mainstream mental model is drain-to-zero; a floor is fill-to-safe. Reusing the grammar of depletion imports the wrong reflex.A fully · C sidesteps it · B keeps the brief
Floor as a region, not a lineA hairline radial tick invites knife-edge anxiety and can’t be labelled. Every working precedent renders the safe state as a band with a dead zone.all three
State in geometry, not hue aloneAmber and sage are reported as indistinguishable in dark mode by users of exactly these apps. Hue-only encoding fails Differentiate Without Color outright.all three
Supply copy is self-referentialThe clinical authority this audience already reads states plainly that pump output is not a valid measure of supply. “Your usual” survives that; “typical” does not.all three — non-negotiable
Repair the paletteAmber 600 is 2.75:1 on cream — it fails even the 3:1 graphical floor, and the dip state is the one that most needs reading.all three
User-declarable suspension statesTwo award-winning apps converged on this independently. A streak that can’t be broken by circumstance can’t be used as a self-punishment instrument.all three · fullest in C
Design for decreasing daily interactionThe competitor is not another tracker, it is abandonment — a deliberate, community-endorsed decision usually taken at 2–8 weeks. A ring plus a daily check-in is two daily obligations.C only — this is C’s thesis
A designed state for flat-or-rising weightA large and vocal cohort gains while nursing. A loop of “stay above the floor and the weight comes off” fails them by design.all three, on Trends
Credential in front of the paywallCredentialing, not features, is the axis this audience uses to decide whether to pay for nutrition advice — and they have been burned by uncredentialed advice.all three, on Onboarding + Paywall

Night mode foreground13.68:114.75:1 with a 20pt type floorThe evidence supports reducing total light, not shifting hue.
One claim to drop from the deck Red-shifted night mode has no melatonin evidence behind it — the trials are null, and individual light sensitivity varies roughly 58-fold between people. Build the dim mode, and defend it on legibility. Never market it as protecting her sleep hormones. The “23 minutes to refocus” statistic is also not in the paper everyone cites; the real cost of an interruption is emotional, not temporal, and the recovery window is 15–20 seconds — which is an argument for never losing state, not for being fast.

What these mocks can’t settle

Questions the design work surfaced but cannot answer without users. Most fold into the discovery interviews already planned — two static mockups each would settle them.

QuestionHow to settle it
Does “still to eat today” land, or feel like a chore?Show A and B side by side. It is the strongest idea here and the least tested in this domain.
Does a self-referential supply statement satisfy the anxiety, or read as the app dodging?The clinical constraint is absolute, so if “steady vs. your own last 7 days” doesn’t satisfy her, the feature needs rethinking — not the copy.
Does low-precision input feel supportive, or feel like not being taken seriously?Two mockups. This decides whether C is viable at all.
Is sage-on-cream distinctive enough?Put B next to Bodily, Expectful and Hey Jane and see whether anyone can tell them apart. That test is the reason A and C exist.
The partner questionShared state with a spouse is the most-cited reason anyone keeps a baby app past week six, and there is no answer to it in this scope. Worth deciding deliberately rather than by default.
How large is the gaining-while-nursing cohort?Recruit at least three interviewees who gained. They will not volunteer.
